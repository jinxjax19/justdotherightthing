Reporting Recipe

How would you do this? If you were a local newsroom looking to have a little fun, try our recipe:

  1. Go to the meeting and record it — good audio quality matters. Take notes. Talk to people.*
  2. Transcribe the recording. We used Descript.
  3. Write an awesome script.**
  4. Find a friend to share your story with — prep them or don't.
  5. Use our code (or your own) to:
    • Pull out key numbers (usually budgets)
    • Separate public comments into pro/con
    • Summarize themes from each side
    • Find the final policy decision and options presented
  6. Check everything. Human-check adds maybe 1 hour — use it.***
  7. Link timestamps to the council's YouTube video so listeners can verify the robot's work.

* You can get audio from the council's website, but you must watch or attend the whole meeting. This is not a shortcut for lazy reporting — it's a recipe for better content.

** Think about what made you laugh, angry, or surprised. Good comedy is full of surprises, and so are good city council meetings.

*** Whenever a human column has content, the code uses the human check over its original output.

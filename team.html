About Us

We met pursuing a master's in journalism at Stanford where we fell head over heels for local news.

Neither of us started out in the news business. Brianna spent her early career in ballet studios and FBI offices. Meanwhile, Ella split her early twenties between school bus depots and multinational non-profits.

We bonded over a love for difficult data, good gossip, and novel news formats, and together, we created Just Do the Right Thing.

We know that local journalism helps us build better communities by keeping you up to date on the mundane policies and practices that shape your daily life. Because seriously, how are we supposed to build a better world if we don't know what's going on in this one?

Brianna wrote the script, edited the video, and told Ella when the website looked bad. Ella co-wrote the script, analyzed the transcripts, designed the website, and used Claude to make it all real.
